Terms & Conditions

Before we make things look good, let's make things clear.

1. Introduction

The Conler is a creative production company based in the Netherlands. We collaborate with brands, agencies, and individuals to produce high-end film, photography, website design, and social media content. Our services range from full project execution to specific support such as cinematography, design, or editing.

2. Scope of Work

Each project is governed by a tailored project brief agreed upon with the client. This document outlines the deliverables, timeline, and responsibilities, and serves as the foundation for the project's execution. Depending on the service involved, specific conditions will be detailed within the project brief.

For website projects, the scope may include:

  • Design and development stages, such as layout and UI/UX
  • CMS configuration, such as WordPress or Shopify
  • Number of pages, plugin setup, and basic SEO structure
  • Optional extras such as e-commerce, hosting, or copywriting

For social media projects, the scope may include:

  • Strategy development and content calendars
  • Post design, writing captions, and hashtag research
  • Scheduling and publishing content
  • Community management, if agreed, and monthly reporting

Payment terms are defined per project and communicated in the proposal. This includes any required deposits before starting and the final payment due upon delivery. Work begins only after the initial payment is received.

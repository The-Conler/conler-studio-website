Sorelle Carboni

Sorelle Carboni is an affittacamere and B&B nestled in Tonara, a mountain village in the heart of Sardinia's Barbagia region. The brand identity was built to reflect the deep roots of the family and the land — drawing from Sardinian folk tradition, carpet motifs, and the warmth of genuine hospitality.

We developed a complete brand system: a rich colour palette of terracotta, sage, teal, and cream, alongside a custom icon library that weaves together Sardinian textile symbols, local nature, and the essence of home.

The project extended into a fully hand-coded website built from scratch in HTML, CSS and JavaScript with GSAP animations, designed to carry the same warmth and identity into the digital experience.

Abu Dhabi Desert Challenge

In April 2024, we partnered with Impeco and W2RC to create exclusive behind-the-scenes content for the Abu Dhabi Desert Challenge. The focus: the complex logistics of transporting racing motorcycles to the desert.

Our role included conducting interviews with key logistics personnel, documenting the entire transport process, and handling all editing and post-production. The final video showcased the precision and teamwork required to prepare for such a high-stakes rally.

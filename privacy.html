Privacy Policy

How we handle the data you share with us.

1. Who we are

The Conler V.O.F. is a creative production studio based in the Netherlands, with active work and contacts in Sardinia, Italy. We are responsible for the personal data processed through our website, contact form, and project communications. You can reach us at conlerevents@gmail.com or by post at Slaperdijk 17, 6846RR Arnhem, Netherlands.

2. What data we collect

We only collect personal data you actively give us, plus minimal technical data needed to keep the site running.

  • Contact form data: name, email address, phone number (optional), and the message you write.
  • Email and call data: the contents of any email, message, or call you initiate with us.
  • Project data: brand assets, briefs, contracts, and any files you share with us as part of a paid engagement.
  • Technical data: standard server logs (IP address, browser type, referrer, timestamps) for security and basic analytics. We do not use third-party advertising or profiling cookies.

3. Why we collect it

We use your personal data to respond to your enquiry, prepare proposals, deliver paid projects, send invoices, and meet legal obligations such as Dutch and EU bookkeeping rules. We do not use your data for marketing without explicit consent.

4. Legal basis

Under the GDPR (Regulation (EU) 2016/679), we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Consent when you submit the contact form or opt into any communication.
  • Performance of a contract when we deliver paid work to you or your business.
  • Legitimate interest for basic site security, fraud prevention, and aggregated analytics.
  • Legal obligation for invoicing and tax records.

5. How long we keep your data

We retain personal data only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected, or as required by Dutch law (typically seven years for financial records). Contact form messages from people who do not become clients are deleted within twelve months. Project files follow the retention rules described in our Terms & Conditions.

6. Who has access

Your data is processed only by The Conler and a limited number of vetted subcontractors who help us deliver projects (e.g. freelance crew, hosting providers, FormSubmit for the contact form). Each is bound by confidentiality and processes data only on our instructions. We do not sell your data to anyone.

7. International transfers

Where data is transferred outside the European Economic Area (for example, hosting providers based in the US), we rely on Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent safeguards required by GDPR.

8. Cookies

This site uses only strictly necessary technical cookies. We do not run third-party advertising, retargeting, or social tracking cookies on our pages. Embedded media (Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, LinkedIn) may set their own cookies when you interact with them — please consult their respective policies.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the processing of your personal data, and the right to data portability. You can exercise any of these rights by emailing conlerevents@gmail.com. We respond within thirty days. You may also lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens) if you believe your rights have been breached.

10. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against loss, misuse, and unauthorised access. This includes encrypted transit (HTTPS), access controls for project files, and limited internal access on a need-to-know basis.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable law. The latest version is always published at this URL with the effective date shown below.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy or about how we handle your data? Write to conlerevents@gmail.com or call +31 6 23 45 92 49.

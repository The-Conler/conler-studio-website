Our story

Two roots. One studio.

The Conler was founded by Sidney and Valentina.

Sidney was born in the Netherlands, but Sardinia was always part of his life. His mother is Italian, and her parents came from Gadoni before moving to the Netherlands for work. Through that family connection, he kept returning to the village.

There, he met Valentina, who was born and raised in Gadoni and has always lived close to the culture, traditions, and identity of the place.

From those two perspectives, The Conler was born: one side connected to movement, structure, and an international view; the other rooted in Sardinia, memory, local culture, and authenticity.

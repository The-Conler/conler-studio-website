Abu Dhabi Desert Challenge

@AbuDhabiDesertChallenge logistics video production storytelling

Brief

In April 2024, we partnered with Impeco and W2RC to create exclusive behind-the-scenes content for the Abu Dhabi Desert Challenge. The focus: the complex logistics of transporting racing motorcycles to the desert.

Our role included conducting interviews with key logistics personnel, documenting the entire transport process, and handling all editing and post-production. The final video, published on the event's official channels, showcased the precision and teamwork required to prepare for such a high-stakes rally.

This collaboration highlighted the often unseen efforts that make world-class motorsport possible, building engagement and offering viewers a new perspective on the challenge beyond the race itself.

— Revealing the journey behind the race.

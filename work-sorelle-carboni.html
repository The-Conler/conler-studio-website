Case Study

Sorelle Carboni

A warm brand identity and hand-coded digital home for an affittacamere rooted in family, Sardinian tradition, and mountain hospitality.

Leghiamo tessuti nel tempo.

We shaped the project around the feeling of continuity: family history, local craft, and the quiet care of a place that welcomes people like home.

Client
Sorelle Carboni
Place
Tonara, Sardinia
Scope
Brand Identity, Photography, Iconography, Web Development

Brand System

Rooted in place

The identity draws from Sardinian textile symbols, mountain colours, family memory, and the tactile details of breakfast, rooms, and shared stories.

#C35F41

Terracotta

For earth, rooftops, handmade materials, and the sun-warmed side of Sardinia.

#FEF1D7

Cream

Used for warmth, breakfast light, and the soft feeling of hospitality.

#99C2AA

Sage

Chosen for mountain freshness, calm interiors, and the quiet green around Tonara.

#2A525F

Teal

A deeper note for heritage, craft, and the graphic strength of the identity.

#8F1934

Cannonau

A rich traditional red for family memory, celebration, and local depth.

#281515

Chestnut

The grounding colour: wood, roots, old interiors, and the weight of history.

Iconography

Symbols from the palette

Five marks — drawn from Sardinian craft, woven textiles and the ritual of breakfast — carry the brand. Each one is rendered in all six palette colours, so the iconography flexes to any surface while always reading as Sorelle Carboni.

The five marks
  • Cornetto
  • Textile flower
  • Woven diamond
  • Window cross
  • Heritage mark
